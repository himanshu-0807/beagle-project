Beagle

The Beagle is a breed of small scent hound, similar in appearance to the much larger Foxhound. Friendly, curious and merry, Beagles are one of the most popular family dogs in the world.

Beagle Trivia

  1. Height – 13–16 in (33–41 cm)
  2. Weight – 22–25 lb (10.0–11.3 kg)
  3. Coat – Short haired, hard coat of medium length
  4. Colour – Tricolor, or white in combination with black & tan or brown & tan
  5. Life span – 12–15 years
